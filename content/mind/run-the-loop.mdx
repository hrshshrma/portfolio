Learn enough to form a smart hypothesis. Run the experiment. Update the model. Repeat.

Pure action without reflection is just thrashing. Pure reflection without action is just dreaming. Progress needs the loop between them.

I have spent plenty of time consuming content, plans, books, and theories. The useful shift is simple: build a small honest thing, let reality answer, and carry that answer into the next attempt.
