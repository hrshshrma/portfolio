I think of life as a fabric with many threads—some bright, some muted, all existing at once.

In any moment, one thread can feel fullest while the others wait their turn. Happy or sad, hurtful or joyful, no single thread makes the life.

The practice is not to deny the thread that is here. It is to see it clearly without mistaking it for the whole fabric.

All the threads together make the life. Keep weaving yours.
